Worship Prep Handbook

Twelve short jobs. Each one has a picture of the real screen with a mark on what to tap. Find the one you need — you don't have to read it all.

Everyone

No sign-in needed

1 Open the app

Works on any phone or computer. Nothing to install.

lhc-prep-tool.vercel.app
TipAdd it to your home screen and it opens like an app. Anyone with the link can look at everything — no password needed.

2 Find your duty

Sundays go across the top. Duties go down the side.

Tap Roster, then read down the column for your Sunday until you find your name. Tap any picture to enlarge it.

AlsoTap the little green WhatsApp circle under a date to send that whole Sunday to a chat.

3 What the colours mean

Most cells are plain. A coloured one is telling you something.

Yellow — not confirmed yet. Still might change, and nothing has gone to anyone's calendar.
✓ Tick — confirmed. Treat it as final.
Red dashed — that person has two duties in the same service. Allowed, but worth a look.
Solid red — a real clash. Someone is double-booked and it needs fixing.

4 Put your duties in your phone calendar

Do it once. Your duties then appear by themselves, and update when the roster changes.

  1. Ask your PIC for your personal calendar link.
  2. Open it and follow the steps for your phone.
  3. Only confirmed duties arrive. Yellow ones stay off until they are signed off.

6 Sign in to make changes

Looking needs nothing. Changing the roster needs a sign-in.

  1. Tap PIC.
  2. Type your phone number. There is no password.
  3. Your name replaces the button. Tap it again to sign out.
If it won't let you inOnly numbers on the PIC list work. Ask another PIC to check the number saved for you.

Drawing — the tick sits inside each yellow cell
  1. Change a name and it turns yellow — a draft.
  2. Tap the small tick inside the cell to confirm just that one. Pressing Enter does the same.
  3. Or use the Confirm button beside the duty name to do that whole month at once.
RememberNothing reaches anyone's calendar until you confirm. If it is still yellow, they have not been told.

8 Update people and their contacts

Open Team on a phone, or Enablers on a computer.

Anyone marked Contact missing cannot be sent a duty card or a calendar link.

  1. Type a phone number or email straight in, then Save Changes.
  2. Duty Card WhatsApps that person their own duties.
  3. Copy Cal Link gives you their calendar link to send.
  4. Check Duplicates now and then for the same person entered twice.
Spelling a name wrong?Use the pencil beside their name in the duty picker. It fixes them everywhere at once. Don't delete and retype — that leaves two half-people.

9 Start a service order

One Sunday's plan. It splits into two halves and you can swap between them any time.

LCD Projection builds the service and drives the screen. Song Order is the music team's list.

  1. Orders, then Create New Order. To carry on with one, tap Open.
  2. Set the date and pick Traditional, Contemporary or Special.
  3. Choose a half.
  4. Save as you go.

10 Put a song in the library

Do it properly once and it is ready for every future service.

Search across the top, the whole library underneath.

  1. Songs, then Add Song, and work along the five steps.
  2. To change one, open it and tap the pencil on its card.
  3. In Lyrics & Chords, put the chords on the line above the words.
Two things people trip on Search matches the start of a title — type the first words, not a word from the middle. And [Verse 1] markers never appear on the screen on Sunday, so use them freely.

11 Put songs into Sunday's order

Three steps: open the library, double-tap the song, tick where it goes.

One — in Song Order, tap Add Songs.

Two — find the song and double-tap it.

Three — tick the part of the service it belongs to, then Add to Selected. The lyrics appear in the Service Order by themselves.

Not sure yet?Add Without Sections drops it into the song list and you can place it later.

12 Run the screen

The black box at the top is exactly what the congregation will see. The service runs underneath it.

Project puts the slide on the big screen; Blank clears it. Not projecting under the slide means the congregation is still seeing nothing.

  1. Click down the order and check nothing is missing.
  2. Read every slide as the congregation will. Use + Add Slide to split a long one.
  3. Project puts it on the screen. Blank clears it between items.
  4. Backgrounds for the preset panels, + Add Media for your own.
PowerPoint and Google Slides Two ways. Upload the file through + Add Media — it takes .pptx, .pdf, .docx, pictures and video. Or paste the Google Slides link, which keeps the layout and the animations and lets you step through slide by slide. For anything with animations, use the link.
Before the service Run the whole order once in full screen on the real projector. It is the only way to catch a background that swallows the text or a video that will not play.
