Worship Prep Handbook

Four short guides. The roster comes in two halves — one for everyone, one for the PICs who edit it — so read the half that is yours and skip the other.

The roster — everyone

No sign-in If you serve on a Sunday, this half is yours.

1 Open the app

Works on any phone or computer. Nothing to install.

lhc-prep-tool.vercel.app
TipAdd it to your home screen and it opens like an app. Anyone with the link can look at everything — no password needed.

2 Find your duty

Sundays go across the top. Duties go down the side.

Tap Roster, then read down the column for your Sunday until you find your name. Tap any picture to enlarge it.

AlsoTap the little green WhatsApp circle under a date to send that whole Sunday to a chat.

3 What the colours mean

Most cells are plain. A coloured one is telling you something.

Yellow — not confirmed yet. Still might change, and nothing has gone to anyone's calendar.
✓ Tick — confirmed. Treat it as final.
Red dashed — that person has two duties in the same service. Allowed, but worth a look.
Solid red — a real clash. Someone is double-booked and it needs fixing.

4 Put your duties in your phone calendar

Do it once. Your duties then appear by themselves, and update when the roster changes.

  1. Ask your PIC for your calendar link. It is personal to you, and it is a different link from the availability one below.
  2. Open it. The page asks for your name, then gives you a one-tap Add to Google Calendar, with Apple and Outlook underneath.
  3. Only confirmed duties arrive. Yellow ones stay off until they are signed off.

5 Tell them when you can't serve

Two taps from the roster. No sign-in, and you don't have to tell anyone — the app takes it straight into next month's planning.

More, then My Availability. On a computer it is Availability, in the row of buttons under the roster.

Can't make one Sunday? Tap that date under Dates I can't serve.
Away for weeks? Put the dates in Away for a stretch and tap Add.
  1. On the Roster, tap More, then My Availability.
  2. Start typing your name and tap it in the list. Two or three letters is enough. Nothing else appears until you have picked one.
  3. Leave This applies to on All duties, or pick one group if you can still do the others.
  4. Tap the Sundays you can't serve. They turn dark red. Tap again to un-mark.
  5. Going away for a while? Put the dates in Away for a stretch and tap Add.
  6. There is nothing to send. Each tap saves as you go, and What you've marked shows where you stand.
  7. The × at the top, or Done, takes you back to the roster.

The same screen also has a link of its own, if your PIC sends you one.

The roster — PICs only

Sign in first Everything here needs the PIC sign-in. Nobody else can do it, or undo it.

1 Sign in as a PIC

Looking needs nothing. Changing the roster needs a sign-in.

  1. Tap PIC.
  2. Type your phone number. There is no password.
  3. Your name replaces the button. Tap it again to sign out.
If it won't let you inOnly numbers on the PIC list work. Ask another PIC to check the number saved for you.

4 Keep people and contacts up to date

Open Team on a phone, or Enablers on a computer.

Anyone marked Contact missing cannot be sent a duty card or a calendar link.

  1. Type a phone number or email straight in, then Save Changes.
  2. Duty Card WhatsApps that person their own duties.
  3. Copy Cal Link gives you their calendar link to send — that is the one from step 4 of the everyone half.
  4. Check Duplicates now and then for the same person entered twice.

5 Availability: send the link, or mark someone away

Your team can now do this themselves from More. This is for sending them the link anyway, and for anyone who will not go looking.

Team is where you send the link from. More is where everyone else marks themselves off.

  1. On the roster, tap Team — Enablers on a computer. The share icon in the green header is Availability link, marked on the picture below.
  2. Tapping it copies the link and opens WhatsApp with a message ready. Send it to the worship group.
  3. It is one link for everyone, and the same one every time. Send it again whenever you are planning a new month — it is a useful nudge even for people who could reach it from More.

Or do it for someone who won't use the link: tap any roster cell, then the calendar icon beside their name.

Sunday's order

Worship team Building the service, and driving the screen on the day.

1 Start a service order

One Sunday's plan. It splits into two halves and you can swap between them any time.

LCD Projection builds the service and drives the screen — that is next. Song Order is the music team's list, after it.

  1. Orders, then Create New Order. To carry on with one, tap Open.
  2. Set the date and pick Traditional, Contemporary or Special.
  3. Choose a half.
  4. Save as you go.

2 LCD Projection — run the screen

The black box at the top is exactly what the congregation will see. The service runs underneath it.

Project puts the slide on the big screen; Blank clears it. Not projecting under the slide means the congregation is still seeing nothing.

  1. Click down the order and check nothing is missing.
  2. Read every slide as the congregation will. Use + Add Slide to split a long one.
  3. Project puts it on the screen. Blank clears it between items.
  4. Backgrounds for the preset panels, + Add Media for your own.
PowerPoint and Google Slides Two ways. Upload the file through + Add Media — it takes .pptx, .pdf, .docx, pictures and video. Or paste the Google Slides link, which keeps the layout and the animations and lets you step through slide by slide. For anything with animations, use the link.
Before the service Run the whole order once in full screen on the real projector. It is the only way to catch a background that swallows the text or a video that will not play.

3 Song Order — put the songs in

Three steps: open the library, double-tap the song, tick where it goes.

One — in Song Order, tap Add Songs.

Two — find the song and double-tap it.

Three — tick the part of the service it belongs to, then Add to Selected. The lyrics appear in the Service Order by themselves.

Not sure yet?Add Without Sections drops it into the song list and you can place it later.

Songs

Anyone can look The library everything else draws from.

1 Look a song up, and check it

Everything the band and the screen use comes from here. Worth a look before Sunday.

Search matches the start of a title — type the first words, not a word from the middle.

  1. Lyrics shows the words as the congregation will see them. Read them through.
  2. Chords shows the same words with the chords above them, for the band.
  3. Add to Order drops it straight into a service without going back to the order.
  4. Share sends it to a chat.
What to check Spelling, the order of the verses, and that the last line is really the last line. A song with no lyrics saved shows nothing on Sunday — the Songs page counts those for you at the top: 59 Lyrics ready out of 63.

2 Add a song, or fix one

Same five steps either way. Do it properly once and it is ready for every future service.

New song: Add Song. Changing one you already have: open it and tap the sliders icon from the step above.

Title and artist, then style and key, then season, then any files — and last, the words.

Step 5 sits at the bottom of the window. 8 section(s), 96 lines under the button tells you something is already saved.

  1. Type or Paste the words in.
  2. Mark each part on its own line: [Chorus], [Verse 1], [Bridge].
  3. Put chords on the line above the words they belong to.
  4. Transpose moves every chord at once. It does not change what is saved as the original key.
  5. Save Lyrics, then Save Changes on the window behind it. Both.
The bit people trip on [Verse 1] markers never appear on the screen on Sunday — they only tell the app where one slide ends and the next begins. Use them freely; a song without them becomes one enormous slide.
